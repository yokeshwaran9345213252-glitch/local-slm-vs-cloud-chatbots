# Presentation Export & Delivery Guide

## 1. Overview
The Capstone 70 presentation deliverable is provided in dual formats:
1. **Interactive HTML5 Slide Deck:** [presentation_slides.html](file:///c:/Users/Dell/OneDrive/Desktop/ai%20llm/08_presentation/presentation_slides.html)
2. **Slide-by-Slide Script & Speaker Notes:** [slide_deck_script.md](file:///c:/Users/Dell/OneDrive/Desktop/ai%20llm/08_presentation/slide_deck_script.md)

---

## 2. Option A: Presenting Directly in the Browser (Recommended)
1. Double click or open `08_presentation/presentation_slides.html` in any web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox).
2. Controls:
   - **Next Slide:** Press `Right Arrow` or `Spacebar`
   - **Previous Slide:** Press `Left Arrow`
   - **Speaker Notes:** Press `N` or click the "Speaker Notes" button
   - **Full Screen:** Click "Fullscreen" or press `F11`
3. The presentation features animated progress indicators, glassmorphic dark styling, responsive card grids, and synchronized speaker notes.

---

## 3. Option B: Exporting to Canva (Free Tier)
1. Log into your free Canva account at [canva.com](https://canva.com).
2. Click **Create a Design** $\rightarrow$ **Presentation (16:9)**.
3. Choose a sleek Dark Mode or Tech template (search: *"AI Technology Deck"* or *"Modern Dark Pitch"*).
4. For each of the 12 slides:
   - Copy the headline, subtitle, and card bullet points from [slide_deck_script.md](file:///c:/Users/Dell/OneDrive/Desktop/ai%20llm/08_presentation/slide_deck_script.md).
   - Paste the presenter script into Canva's **Presenter Notes** section at the bottom of each slide.
5. Export as **PDF Standard** or present live via Canva's Presenter View.

---

## 4. Option C: Exporting to Google Slides
1. Open [Google Slides](https://slides.new).
2. Choose **Theme** $\rightarrow$ **Simple Dark** or **Streamline**.
3. Create 12 slides corresponding to the 12 sections in `slide_deck_script.md`.
4. Copy the Speaker Notes into the **Speaker Notes** tray below each slide.
5. Click **Slideshow** $\rightarrow$ **Presenter View** to view the current slide, next slide preview, and speaker timer.
